Vertical Shift D — The Wave's New Home Base
y = A·sin(x) + D. A daily temperature cycle: 15°C average, swinging ±8°C. Drag D and watch the ENTIRE wave slide up, oscillating around its new center line.
D doesn't change the SHAPE of the oscillation (still swings ±8°) — it only relocates where "zero" of the oscillation sits. This is exactly why climatologists model daily temperature as sin(time) scaled and shifted: the sine captures the swing, D captures the season's average.
