Law of Cosines: Pythagoras, With a Correction Term
c² = a² + b² − 2ab·cos(C). Drag angle C toward 90° in the actual triangle — watch the correction term vanish, leaving pure Pythagoras.
When C=90°, cos(C)=0, so the correction term −2ab·cos(C) vanishes entirely, leaving exactly c²=a²+b² — the ordinary Pythagorean theorem. Watch the triangle itself become a right triangle exactly at C=90°.
