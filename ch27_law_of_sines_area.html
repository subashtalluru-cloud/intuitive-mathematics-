Law of Sines — Born From Computing Area Twice
Same triangle, same area — computed two different ways using two different side/angle pairs. Click through to see why sin(A)/a = sin(B)/b falls out for free.
This is a genuinely different derivation technique from the Law of Cosines (which came from the Pythagorean theorem). The Law of Sines comes from a completely independent idea: the SAME triangle's area, computed two consistent ways, must agree.
