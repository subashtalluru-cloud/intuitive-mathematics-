The Discriminant — Predicting the Parabola's Shape
Adjust a, b, c in ax²+bx+c=0. Watch how the discriminant b²−4ac's sign alone predicts whether the parabola crosses the x-axis twice, once, or not at all.
Not because the algebra fails when the discriminant is negative — but because no real crossing point genuinely exists. Chapter 60's complex numbers eventually give these "missing" solutions a home.
