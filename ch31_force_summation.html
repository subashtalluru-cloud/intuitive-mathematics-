A Bridge Cable Under Three Forces at Once
Weight, wind, and traffic load all pull on this cable simultaneously, each with its own magnitude and direction. Drag any force vector — the net force (bold black) recomputes instantly as their tip-to-tail sum.
Attempting this with separately-tracked magnitude and direction numbers would require constant, error-prone conversion. Vector addition handles the combination automatically and correctly, because each vector's components already encode both pieces of information in directly combinable form.
