One Harmonic Alone: Shape Frozen, Only Size Shrinks
Initial condition is already a single pure sine — no other frequencies to compete with. Drag time forward and watch the curve shrink in place, its exact sine shape never distorting.
This shape-preserving behavior is a special property of a SINGLE-harmonic initial condition — a general profile with multiple frequencies will have its shape itself distort over time, not merely shrink uniformly.
