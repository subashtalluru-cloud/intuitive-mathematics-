One Dial, Four Curves
Eccentricity e is a single number measuring how far a conic deviates from a perfect circle. Slide it from 0 to beyond 1 and watch the SAME underlying shape smoothly become a circle, an ellipse, a parabola, and finally a hyperbola — not four unrelated curves, one continuous family.
e=0e=0.5e=1.0e=1.5
At exactly e=1, something qualitatively different happens: the curve transitions from closed (ellipse) to open (parabola) — one focus has, in a precise sense, been pushed infinitely far away.
