Approaching the Asymptote — f(x) = 1/(x−2)
Click values of x getting closer and closer to 2. Watch what happens to f(x) — and notice x=2 itself is simply excluded, not "close to undefined."
Only the EXACT zero of the denominator (x=2) is excluded from the domain. Every nearby point, however close, is perfectly well-defined — the function just grows unboundedly large as you approach.
