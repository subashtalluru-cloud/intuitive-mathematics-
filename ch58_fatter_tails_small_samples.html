A Brewer's Small-Sample Problem — Fatter Tails, Honestly Earned
n=9, mean=52, s=6, testing against a claimed mean of 48. The z-test (assuming known sigma) would reject. The correctly-adjusted t-test, honest about small-sample uncertainty, does not. Drag degrees of freedom and watch the t-distribution converge toward the normal curve.
The t-distribution's extra tail-heaviness isn't fixed — it directly reflects how much data went into estimating the standard deviation, converging smoothly to the normal distribution as that estimate becomes more reliable.
