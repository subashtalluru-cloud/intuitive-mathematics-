Equivalent Fractions — Same Pizza, Different Cuts
Drag the slider to change how many slices the pizza is cut into. The shaded fraction always represents the same amount of pizza.
3/4, 6/8, 9/12, 12/16 all shade the exact same area — the denominator says how many equal pieces the whole is cut into, the numerator says how many you take. Cross-multiplication (3×8 = 6×4 = 24) confirms it numerically without drawing anything.
