From Notches to Numbers: A 32,000-Year Walk
Drag the marker (or click a dot) along the timeline to see how "a collection of things" slowly became "an abstract, portable idea."
Each stop is a real, physical increase in abstraction — from "one notch per sheep" (still tied to specific sheep) to "the symbol 5" (tied to nothing at all). That escalating detachment from any one physical situation is the thread running through all of Chapter 1.
