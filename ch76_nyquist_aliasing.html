8,000 Hz Sampling Exceeds a 5,000 Hz Signal — Still Not Enough
Nyquist requires DOUBLE the highest frequency, not merely "faster than" it. Drag the sampling rate and watch exactly when aliasing distortion kicks in.
Aliasing, once it has occurred during sampling, cannot be corrected or reversed afterward by any subsequent digital processing — preventing it requires acting proactively before sampling, not attempting cleanup afterward.
