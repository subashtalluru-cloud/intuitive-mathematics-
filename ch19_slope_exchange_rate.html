Constant Rate vs. Changing Rate — Click Along the Path
Click points along the ramp, then along the stairs. Measure "rise over run" at each spot — does the rate ever change?
A linear function's slope is a fixed exchange rate — like "3 euros per dollar," true no matter how many dollars are exchanged. 10 dollars always yields 30 euros; 1000 always yields 3000. No volume discount, no surcharge, ever.
