The Dedekind Cut — A Knife Blade Along the Rationals
Drag the blade. Every rational number sorts cleanly into "left of the blade" (L) or "right of the blade" (U). Watch what happens when the blade rests exactly at √2.
Move the blade to exactly 1.41421356 (as close as the slider allows) — you'll never find a rational sitting exactly ON the blade there. That gap-position, precisely specified by which rationals fall left vs. right, IS the real number √2 — defined into existence, not discovered as already sitting somewhere waiting.
