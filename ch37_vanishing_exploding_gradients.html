Why Deep Neural Networks Are Hard to Train — Chain Rule, Repeated 50 Times
Each network layer has its own local rate. Backpropagation multiplies them ALL together via the chain rule, applied repeatedly. Drag the per-layer rate and the layer count — watch the combined gradient vanish toward zero or explode toward infinity.
Recognizing vanishing and exploding gradients as direct, inevitable numerical consequences of the chain rule applied repeatedly across many layers is genuinely valuable diagnostic insight.
