Measure What's Accessible, Compute What Isn't
You can't climb the tree to measure its height. You CAN measure the ground distance and the angle up to its top. Trigonometry bridges the gap.
Hipparchus built the first trigonometric tables for exactly this reason: celestial distances are entirely inaccessible to direct measurement, while angular positions are directly observable. GPS, land surveying, and celestial navigation are all the identical accessible-angle-to-inaccessible-distance translation.
