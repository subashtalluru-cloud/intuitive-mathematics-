Pairing Infinities — Naturals vs. Evens
Click "Extend" to pair more numbers. Every natural number n pairs with the even number 2n. Watch: does anyone ever get left without a partner?

Even numbers (proper subset of ℕ)
Intuition screams that "evens" should be half of "naturals" — evens are a proper subset, after all. But the bijection rule n ↔ 2n pairs every single natural with exactly one even, forever, with none left over on either side. By the pairing definition of size, the two sets are exactly the same size: both infinite, both "countably infinite."
