"If the Moon Is Cheese, Then 2+2=5" — Why This Is TRUE
This sounds absurd, but it's true by the conditional's own definition. Click to test the promise-contract — watch whether it ever actually gets triggered.
Think of P→Q as a PROMISE: "IF P happens, THEN I guarantee Q." A promise is only BROKEN when P happens and Q fails to follow. If P never happens, the promise was never tested — and an untested promise cannot have been broken.
