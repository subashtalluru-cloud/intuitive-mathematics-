Cosine Doesn't Care About Sign. Sine Does.
Drag the angle. Watch θ (blue) and −θ (orange) mirror across the x-axis — same x-coordinate, opposite y-coordinate.
Flipping the angle's sign reflects the swept point across the x-axis. The x-coordinate (cosine) stays put — that's what "even" means. The y-coordinate (sine) flips — that's what "odd" means. This single symmetry derives every subtraction formula from the addition formula.
