Six "Independent" 5% Tests Are Not a 5% Risk Overall
Comparing 4 groups pairwise means 6 separate t-tests, each with its own 5% false-positive chance. Drag the number of groups and watch the OVERALL chance that at least one comparison shows a fake "significant" difference — even with zero real group differences.
This is not merely "more detailed" than ANOVA — running many uncorrected pairwise tests is a genuine methodological error that inflates the true false-positive rate well beyond the researcher's own intended significance level.
