The Skeptic's Challenge — Can You Guarantee the Closeness?
f(x)=(x^2-4)/(x-2) approaches 4 as x approaches 2. A skeptic demands: get f(x) within epsilon of 4. Shrink epsilon and drag delta — watch whether your response band ACTUALLY keeps every output inside the target band.
This challenge-and-response structure — skeptic proposes an arbitrarily small target band, you must guarantee a delta that keeps every output inside it — is exactly what the formal epsilon-delta definition encodes symbolically.
