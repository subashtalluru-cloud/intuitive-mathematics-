The #1 Calculator Trap: Degree Mode vs Radian Mode
Compute sin(30) in both modes — watch the unit circle show why these give wildly different (and both "correct," for their own mode) answers.
Typing "sin(30)" means two completely different sweeps depending on mode — 30° is a small sweep near the start; 30 radians is nearly 5 full rotations. This single setting mismatch is one of the most common real errors in applied trigonometry.
