A Circle Fails the Vertical Line Test — But Still Has a Tangent Slope
x^2+y^2=25 isn't an explicit function (Chapter 17's own vertical line test fails). Drag a point around the circle and watch implicit differentiation find the tangent slope anyway, at every single point.
Differentiating x^2+y^2=25 with respect to x gives 2x+2y(dy/dx)=0, treating y as an implicit function of x and applying the chain rule at every y term - solved directly: dy/dx=-x/y.
