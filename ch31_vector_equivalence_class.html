Same Vector, Infinitely Many Places to Draw It
Click "Relocate" repeatedly. Each click draws vector (3,4) starting from a completely different, randomly chosen point — different pixels on screen every time, but click "Check identity" to confirm they're all secretly the same object.
A vector is best understood not as a picture but as an equivalence class of arrows — directly echoing Chapter 9's fractions as equivalence classes of "cut into n pieces, take k" instructions.
