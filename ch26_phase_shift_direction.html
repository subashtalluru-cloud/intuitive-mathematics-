"x − φ" Shifts RIGHT — The Counterintuitive Sign
y = sin(x − φ). Feels like subtracting should move things left. Click to see exactly why it's the opposite.
The shifted function reproduces the ORIGINAL's starting behavior not at x=0, but at x=φ — precisely where (x−φ) equals zero. If φ is positive, that "starting point" has been delayed to a later, more rightward x-value. The subtraction is what accomplishes the delay.
