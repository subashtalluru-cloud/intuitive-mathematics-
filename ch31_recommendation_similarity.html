Your Taste Is a Vector — Recommendations Are Just Dot Products
Set your own preference for Action, Romance, and Comedy (0-5 each). Each movie is also a vector of genre scores. Watch the dot-product-based similarity rank movies live as you drag.
Representing a user's preferences as a vector of numerical features, and using the dot product (normalized appropriately) to measure how closely two such vectors align, is a foundational technique underlying many real recommendation algorithms.
