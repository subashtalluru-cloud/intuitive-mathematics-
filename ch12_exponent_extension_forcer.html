Why 2⁰ = 1 and 2⁻¹ = 1/2 — Forced, Not Chosen
The pattern of halving as you step left is not a convention — it's the ONLY way to keep the rule 2ᵃ×2ᵇ = 2^(a+b) consistent. Click to extend the pattern leftward.
Each step left divides by 2 — not by choice, but because 2³=8, and to keep the pattern "each step right doubles" consistent when reversed, each step left must halve. This same forcing logic derives 2⁰=1, 2⁻¹=1/2, and ultimately fractional exponents as roots.
