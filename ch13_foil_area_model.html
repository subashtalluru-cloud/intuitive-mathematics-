FOIL Isn't a Trick — It's Four Rectangles

FOIL (First, Outer, Inner, Last) is just a systematic way of naming these four non-overlapping pieces in the order you'd naturally read them off the picture. Total area = sum of the four pieces, no overlaps, no gaps — that's why the algebra must work out.
