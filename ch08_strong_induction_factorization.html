Strong Induction in Action — Prime Factorization
Ordinary induction assumes only "true at k" to prove "true at k+1." Strong induction assumes ALL of 1...k at once. Watch why factorization needs the strong version.
Proving "every integer > 1 has a prime factorization" by ordinary induction would need to relate n+1 back to n specifically — but n+1's factors are usually NOT close to n's. Strong induction, assuming the claim for ALL smaller numbers at once, is the natural tool here.
