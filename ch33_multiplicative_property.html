Scale Twice in a Row — Does It Multiply?
Set two independent scaling factors. Compare "scale by A, then scale by B" against "scale once by A×B combined" — watch the two areas track identically no matter what you set them to.
This is exactly why a rotation matrix always has determinant exactly 1 — a rotation never stretches area, so applying the Pythagorean identity confirms its own scaling factor must equal 1, algebraically and always.
