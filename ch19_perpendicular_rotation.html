Why Perpendicular Slopes Are Negative Reciprocals
A line with slope 2. Click "Rotate 90°" and watch the rise/run triangle rotate along with it.
Not a fact to memorize — a direct consequence of geometry. A 90° rotation turns "rise 2, run 1" into "rise 1, run −2" (the horizontal leg flips direction) — giving new slope = 1/(−2) = −1/2, the exact negative reciprocal.
