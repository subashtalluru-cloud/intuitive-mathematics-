Same Wave, Offset by a Quarter-Turn
Watch sine and cosine graphed side by side as the angle sweeps. Notice: they're the EXACT same shape, just shifted.
Cosine's horizontal position at any angle is exactly what sine's vertical position will be one quarter-turn LATER — because a quarter-turn rotation of the circle swaps horizontal and vertical extent completely. This isn't coincidence to memorize; it's the direct seed of Chapter 25's phase-shift identities.
