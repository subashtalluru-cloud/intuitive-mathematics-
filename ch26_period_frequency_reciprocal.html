Period and Frequency Are Reciprocals — Watch Why
Frequency = cycles per second. Period = seconds per cycle. Drag the frequency slider and watch the period (measured directly on the wave) shrink exactly as 1/f predicts.
This isn't a coincidence to memorize — if a wave completes f cycles every second, then simple division tells you each individual cycle must take 1/f seconds. Confusing "frequency" with "ω" (angular frequency, used inside the sine function) is the chapter's own flagged common mistake — they differ by a factor of 2π.
