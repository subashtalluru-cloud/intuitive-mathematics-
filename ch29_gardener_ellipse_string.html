The Gardener's Ellipse — Pull the String Taut, Anywhere
Two pins (foci) are fixed. A loop of string, always exactly 10 units total, connects them through your pencil. Drag the pencil anywhere — the string automatically stays taut, and the two segment lengths always sum to 10, no matter where you pull.
Drag the pencil to a spot exactly between the two pins — watch the two foci themselves merge in your mind's eye as the ellipse's own defining sum-condition becomes a circle's single-distance condition instead.
