Discount Then Tax, or Tax Then Discount?
$100 item, 20% discount, 8% tax. Try both order combinations — first with a percentage discount, then a flat $10 discount. Watch the bars.
A retailer who concludes "order never matters" from Case A alone would be systematically wrong on every Case-B-style flat-discount transaction. The bars make it visible directly: identical heights mean order truly doesn't matter; different heights mean it does.
