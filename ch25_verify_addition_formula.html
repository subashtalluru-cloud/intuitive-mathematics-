The Real Formula — Two Rotations, One Landing Point
Watch angle A swept first (light), then angle B swept further (dark) — landing at A+B. The addition formula predicts exactly this final point's coordinates.
The two-part sweep (A, then further by B) lands at exactly the same point as sweeping straight to A+B — and that landing point's coordinates are exactly what sin(A)cos(B)+cos(A)sin(B) computes.
