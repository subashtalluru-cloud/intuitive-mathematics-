Regrouping Is Free. Reordering Is Not.
f(x)=x+1, g(x)=2x, h(x)=x². Click to see (f∘g)∘h vs f∘(g∘h) — same physical sequence, different bracket — then compare against actually reversing the order.
Regrouping three machines in an unchanged sequence never alters the physical processing order — it's just a human bookkeeping bracket. Reordering actually swaps which machine processes first. This is why composition is associative (grouping is free) but NOT commutative (order is not free).
