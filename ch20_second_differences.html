The Rate of Change of the Rate of Change
f(x) = x². Click to compute successive rates of change — watch the bars for "rate" grow, but the bars for "Δrate" (second difference) all stay the SAME height.
The sequence of local rates (1, 3, 5, 7...) is itself increasing at a constant rate — a linear pattern one level removed. This "differences of differences being constant" is the exact discrete ancestor of the second derivative (Chapter 41).
