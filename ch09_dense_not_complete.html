Dense Doesn't Mean "No Gaps" — A Trap Worth Falling Into Once
The rationals are dense — between any two, infinitely many more exist. Click to zoom the number line closer and closer to √2. Watch the rationals crowd in — but never quite land on it.
This is Chapter 9's own flagged common mistake: assuming density means the rationals have no gaps. Chapter 11 proves this assumption false — density (infinitely many rationals nearby) and completeness (no missing points at all) are genuinely different properties.
