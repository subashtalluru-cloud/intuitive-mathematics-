The Determinant Is Just the Area You're Looking At
Drag either basis vector. The shaded parallelogram's actual area, computed independently by geometry, and the algebraic determinant formula ad−bc, always match — try dragging one vector onto the other's line and watch both hit zero together.
A determinant of 0 collapses everything to zero area — a singular matrix, permanently unable to be undone, echoing Chapter 22's injectivity requirement.
