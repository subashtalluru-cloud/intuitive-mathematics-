The Equation Doesn't Know Which Answer You Want
A ball is launched: h(t) = −5t² + 20t + 1. Click to see the trajectory graph AND both roots of h(t)=0 — one is the real landing time, the other is before launch.
Solving correctly is necessary but not sufficient for answering the real-world question. A negative-time solution is mathematically valid — it satisfies the equation exactly — but physically meaningless, visible directly as sitting to the LEFT of the graph's start.
