Two Models Agree at First — Then One Predicts Impossibly More Animals Than the Reserve Can Hold
Carrying capacity K=5000, starting population 50, growth rate 0.4/year. Drag time forward and watch pure exponential growth barrel straight past the reserve's physical limit while the logistic model correctly bends and levels off.
The exponential model remains reasonably accurate only while the population stays genuinely small relative to K — using it once the population approaches carrying capacity produces a manifestly unrealistic result.
