Amplitude and Frequency — Two Genuinely Independent Knobs
y = A·sin(Bx). Adjust A and B separately — watch how changing one never affects the other.

A appears OUTSIDE the sine function, scaling only vertical height. B appears INSIDE, scaling only how fast the angle accumulates. Because they occupy structurally different positions in the formula, adjusting either one provably cannot affect the other — confirmed directly from the formula's structure, not just from observing a few examples.
