Where Does the Plane Actually Go? — Heading Plus Wind
Drag the pilot's intended heading (green) and the wind's own push (orange). The actual ground track (purple dashed) is nothing but their vector sum — drag the wind vector opposite the heading and watch the plane's real speed drop.
A pilot describing this as "flying 400mph" while ignoring wind would be dangerously wrong about their actual ground speed and direction — exactly the single-number inadequacy vectors exist to fix.
