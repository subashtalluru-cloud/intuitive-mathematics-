When the Curve Dips Below the Axis — Area Turns Negative
f(x)=x-1 from 0 to 3. Part of this region sits BELOW the x-axis. Click to reveal how the integral handles this: negative area subtracts, rather than simply adding like ordinary geometric area.
A definite integral is not the same thing as "area between curve and axis" in the everyday geometric sense — it is SIGNED area, where regions below the axis contribute negatively to the total.
