Drag Just Two Vectors — Predict Where Every Other Vector Goes
Drag where the basis vectors i and j land. A third test vector (3,2) is NOT draggable — watch it get repositioned automatically, purely computed from where you put i and j. That computation is literally what a matrix is.
Knowing only where i and j land is genuinely sufficient to determine the transformation's effect on every other vector — since every vector is some combination of these two basis directions, and the transformation must respect that combination.
