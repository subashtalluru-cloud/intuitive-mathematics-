Series wiring forces current through BOTH switches — exactly AND's truth table. Parallel wiring lets current through EITHER path — exactly OR's truth table. This is why Claude Shannon's 1937 thesis (age 21) connecting Boolean algebra to switching circuits became the direct ancestor of digital electronics.
