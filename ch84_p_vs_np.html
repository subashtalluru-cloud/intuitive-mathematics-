Easy to Check. Nobody Knows If It's Easy to Solve.
Try solving a genuinely hard puzzle yourself, then try just checking a given answer. The dramatic difference in effort between these two tasks is exactly what "P versus NP" asks about — and nobody has ever proven whether that gap is fundamental or illusory.
Modern cryptography depends directly on the widespread, strong suspicion that P does not equal NP — yet no one has ever rigorously proven this suspicion true, nor found a counterexample disproving it.
