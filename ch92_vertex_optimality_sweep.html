Sweep a Line Across the Triangle — It Always Leaves Through a Corner
Maximize 3x+2y subject to x\u22650, y\u22650, x+y\u22646. Drag the objective value and watch the parallel line sweep the feasible region — the very last point of contact is always a vertex, never an interior point.
This vertex-optimality guarantee makes linear programming especially tractable — rather than checking infinitely many interior points, checking only the finitely many corner vertices is fully, provably sufficient.
