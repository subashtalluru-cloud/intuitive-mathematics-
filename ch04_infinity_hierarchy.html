Cantor's diagonal argument proves the reals form a strictly LARGER infinity than the naturals — a "countable" infinity (ℕ, evens, even the rationals) is a fundamentally different size than an "uncountable" one (ℝ). Infinity isn't one thing; it's a whole hierarchy of different sizes.
