Belief, Refined Draw by Draw — Never Restarting From Scratch
A jar is equally likely to be 70%-red or 70%-blue. Draw marbles one at a time; each posterior becomes the next prior. Watch belief climb, compound, and eventually cross into near-certainty.
A Bayesian credible interval can genuinely be interpreted as "there is a 95% probability the true value lies within this range" — precisely the interpretation Chapter 56 carefully ruled out for the frequentist confidence interval.
