Where 30-60-90's "Nice" Values Actually Come From
sin(30°)=1/2 looks like it was just handed down. Click to see the equilateral-triangle construction that actually produces it.
An equilateral triangle (all sides equal, all angles 60°) splits perfectly in half via its own altitude, creating a 30-60-90 triangle where the short leg is EXACTLY half the hypotenuse — that's where sin(30°)=1/2 comes from. Not arbitrary — geometrically forced.
