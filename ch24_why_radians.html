A Radian IS a Distance — Not an Arbitrary Unit
Degrees divide a circle into 360 arbitrary parts (Babylonian base-60 convenience). Radians measure something physically real: the arc length itself. Click to trace it.
On the unit circle (radius 1), the ratio "arc length / radius" simplifies to just the arc length itself. A radian-measured angle IS, literally, the distance traveled along the circle — no conversion factor, no arbitrary division into 360 parts.
