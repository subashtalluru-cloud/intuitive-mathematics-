Two Machines, Two Local Rates — They Multiply, Not Add
g(x)=(3x+1)^4 at x=1. The inner machine (3x+1) and outer machine (u^4) each have their own local amplification rate. Drag x and watch the combined rate always equal the PRODUCT of the two, never their sum.
Inner: u=3x+1
local rate = 3
->
Outer: g=u^4
local rate = 4u^3
This is exactly why the chain rule multiplies rather than adds — a small nudge to x produces an amplified nudge to u, which the outer machine then amplifies again, compounding the two effects multiplicatively.
