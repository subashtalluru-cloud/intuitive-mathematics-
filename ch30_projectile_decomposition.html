A Projectile Is Two Independent Stories, Not One Diagonal One
Watch the SAME thrown ball as three synchronized views: horizontal motion alone (constant velocity, Chapter 19's linear rule), vertical motion alone (constant acceleration, Chapter 20's quadratic rule), and the combined trajectory they produce together.
