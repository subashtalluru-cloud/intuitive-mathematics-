Checking an Answer vs. Finding One — Genuinely Different Difficulty
y'=2y. Compare how much work it takes to CONFIRM a candidate answer versus DISCOVER one from nothing but the bare equation.
VERIFY (given y=Ce^2x)
1. Differentiate: y' = 2Ce^2x
2. Compare to 2y = 2Ce^2x
3. Match. Done.
Tools needed: Chapter 36's mechanical rules only.
SOLVE (given only y'=2y)
1. No candidate answer to check.
2. Requires: separate variables, integrate both sides, exponentiate.
3. A genuinely new technique (Chapter 48), not yet available here.
Tools needed: an entire new chapter.
This exact asymmetry — checking is easy, finding is hard — reappears directly in root-finding (evaluating a function is easy, finding where it equals zero is hard) and in computational complexity (verifying a proposed solution is often far easier than discovering one).
