SOH-CAH-TOA Is a Special Case, Not the Real Definition
Right-triangle ratios (opposite/hypotenuse) and unit-circle coordinates should agree whenever BOTH apply. Click to verify.
"Sine/cosine only apply to angles 0-90°" is a genuine misconception — the unit-circle definition applies to ANY angle. Right-triangle ratios are a restricted special case that happens to agree with it, only where a legitimate triangle can actually be drawn.
