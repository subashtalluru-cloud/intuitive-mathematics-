60 Rolls, Not Exactly 10 of Each Face — Is This Die Rigged?
A fair die predicts about 10 rolls per face out of 60. Actual counts: 12, 8, 11, 9, 13, 7. Each bar's real, unscaled deviation is shown — but the chi-square statistic scales each one by the expected count first.
A commonly cited rule of thumb requires expected counts of at least 5 in each cell for the chi-square approximation to remain reliable — with smaller expected counts, alternative methods like Fisher's exact test are more appropriate.
