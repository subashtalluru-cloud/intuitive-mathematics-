Walking the Number Line
Start at 8. Each button is a step: "+3" walks 3 steps right (east); "−5" walks 5 steps left (west). Watch where you land.
Starting position: 8
8 − 5 = 3: walking 5 steps west from 8 lands on 3. Notice subtracting is just "walk west" — and this is exactly why 8 + (−5) gives the identical landing spot as 8 − 5. Subtraction IS addition of the opposite.
