Divide by n or n-1? A Small Change With a Real Consequence
Sample variance divides by n-1, not n — Bessel's correction. Drag the sample size and watch how much this single choice matters, and how that gap shrinks as your sample grows.
This is not a pedantic technicality — dividing by n instead of n-1 is a systematic bias that consistently UNDERESTIMATES the true population variance, most severely for small samples.
