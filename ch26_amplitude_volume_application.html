Amplitude = Volume, Frequency = Pitch
An audio engineer's mixing board is really just amplitude and frequency knobs. Adjust both and watch — and see — how they map onto what you'd hear.

A louder sound is a bigger amplitude (taller wave) — more air pressure variation. A higher-pitched sound is a higher frequency (more compressed wave) — faster air pressure oscillation. These are the SAME two independent knobs Chapter 26's formula names A and B, just relabeled for human ears.
