Between Any Two Rationals, Another Always Exists
Start with 1/3 and 1/2. Click "Find the midpoint" repeatedly. Does this process ever run out of room?
The rationals have no "next" number — between any two, however close, their average is always a new rational strictly in between. This is called density, and it's a genuinely different property from the whole numbers, which DO have a "next" (5 is immediately followed by 6, with nothing in between).
