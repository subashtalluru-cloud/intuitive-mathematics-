XOR — The Hallway Light With Two Switches
A real hallway light: flip EITHER switch alone, and the light changes state. Click each switch and watch the wire diagram light up (or not).
This exact gate is the working core of a hallway light with two switches, and of binary addition's carry-free digit sum — XOR detects "exactly one is true," a genuinely different question from AND's "both" or OR's "at least one."
