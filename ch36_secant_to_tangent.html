Watch a Secant Line Pivot Into a Tangent Line
f(x)=x^2. Fixed point at x=2. Slide the second point closer and closer — watch the secant's slope converge to exactly 4, the power rule's own prediction (f'(x)=2x, so f'(2)=4).
This numerical convergence is not a coincidence specific to this one example — it's the limit machinery from the previous chapter, made fully concrete and hand-verifiable.
