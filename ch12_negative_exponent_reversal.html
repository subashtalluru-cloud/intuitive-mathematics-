Negative Exponent = "Do It, Then Reverse It"
2³ means "scale by 8." 2⁻³ means the SAME scaling, then a reversal. Click to watch a bar physically stretch, then shrink back — landing exactly where it started.
If "×2³" means "scale by 8," then "×2⁻³" scales by 8, then reverses — i.e. divides by 8. Two reversals of the same operation cancel exactly, landing you back where you started. This directly explains why 2⁻ⁿ×2ⁿ=1, without treating it as a separate rule.
