A 99% Accurate Test — Only 16.7% Chance You're Actually Sick
1% of the population has a rare disease. A test is 99% accurate for true positives, 95% accurate for true negatives. You test positive. Drag the disease's own rarity and watch how dramatically that changes what your positive result actually means.
Confusing P(positive|disease) with P(disease|positive) — the exact error Bayes' theorem corrects — is precisely the error that has caused real wrongful courtroom convictions and genuine patient panic over false-positive medical results.
