No Laurent Series Required — A Simple Pole's Residue Is Just a Limit
f(z) = e^z/(z-2) has a pole at z=2. The full theory says find the Laurent series' 1/(z-a) coefficient — but for a simple pole, a direct limit shortcut skips that entirely.
Both worked examples in this chapter apply this direct, practical formula successfully, without ever needing to invoke the full Laurent series machinery the general definition initially introduces.
