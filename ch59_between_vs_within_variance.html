Same Three Group Means — Drag the Spread, Watch F Collapse
Three groups with means 50, 55, 60 (n=10 each). Drag each group's own internal spread and watch the F-statistic — comparing spread BETWEEN groups to spread WITHIN groups — change dramatically, even though the means never move.
A large F-statistic (between-group variance well exceeding within-group variance) signals genuine group differences; F near 1 signals the apparent differences are entirely consistent with ordinary noise.
