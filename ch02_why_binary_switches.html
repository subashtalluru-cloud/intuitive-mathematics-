Why Binary? A Switch Only Needs Two Reliable States
Imagine building a "base-10 switch" that reliably distinguishes 10 different voltage levels vs. a "base-2 switch" that only needs to distinguish ON from OFF. Click to compare reliability.

A base-2 switch needs to distinguish just 2 states — trivially reliable even with electrical noise. A base-10 switch needs 10 distinct, evenly-spaced voltage levels — genuinely hard to build reliably. This physical engineering constraint, not mathematical preference, is why binary won.
