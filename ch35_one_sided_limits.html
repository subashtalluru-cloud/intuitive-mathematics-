Approach From the Left, Approach From the Right — Do They Meet?
A two-sided limit exists only if BOTH one-sided limits agree. Toggle between a piecewise function that connects smoothly and one deliberately mismatched — watch the visible jump appear exactly where the one-sided limits disagree.
A genuine, visible jump in a function's graph corresponds exactly to disagreeing one-sided limits — this is the precise diagnostic tool for spotting discontinuities that later numerical methods must handle with special care.
