R₀=2 Needs Half the Population Immune. R₀=15 Needs Almost Everyone.
The herd immunity threshold grows dramatically FASTER than R₀ itself. Drag R₀ and watch the required immune fraction climb — not proportionally, but with sharply diminishing room left as R₀ rises.
Herd immunity is a population-level, statistical statement about an epidemic's overall sustained growth capacity — not an individual-level guarantee; susceptible individuals remain individually vulnerable even after the threshold is reached.
