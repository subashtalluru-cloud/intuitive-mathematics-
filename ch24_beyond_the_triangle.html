A Triangle Can't Have a 200° Angle — But the Circle Doesn't Care
Click each angle and watch the point sweep around the unit circle. Past 90°, the "triangle" (dashed) disappears — but the point, and its coordinates, remain perfectly well-defined.
A triangle-based definition inherently requires the angle to sit strictly between 0° and 90°. The unit-circle definition places no such restriction — the swept point, and its (cos θ, sin θ) coordinates, stay well-defined at every angle, including ones no triangle could ever have.
