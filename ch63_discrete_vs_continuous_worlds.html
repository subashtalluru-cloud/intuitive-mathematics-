Zoom In on the Integers — Nothing New Appears. Zoom In on the Reals — Infinity Does.
Click "zoom in" repeatedly on each number line. One reveals genuinely new numbers at every scale, forever. The other never does — its gaps are real and permanent.
This isn't a minor technicality — it's exactly why discrete mathematics needs an entirely different toolkit from calculus: derivatives and continuous integrals were built for the dense, gapless world; counting and graph structure were built for the gapped, countable one.
