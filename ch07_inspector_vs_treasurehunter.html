The Inspector vs. The Treasure Hunter
10 crates, each holds a number. Claim: "every crate holds an even number" (∀). Claim: "some crate holds a number over 50" (∃). Watch the meter — one claim is fragile (one failure ends it), the other is resilient (one success ends it).
The Inspector (∀) can be defeated by ONE bad crate but needs to open every crate to declare victory. The Treasure Hunter (∃) can declare victory on ONE good crate but needs to open every crate to declare defeat. They are structural mirror images.
