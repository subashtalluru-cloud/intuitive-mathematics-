Fold a Paper — How Tall Does It Get?
A sheet of paper is 0.1mm thick. Each fold doubles the layer count. Click to fold and watch how catastrophically the height outpaces intuition.
0 folds: 1 layer, 0.1mm thick
Folded 42 times (physically impossible, but mathematically well-defined), the stack's height would exceed the distance from Earth to the Moon. Each fold adds 1 to the exponent — but the HEIGHT doesn't grow by a fixed amount, it doubles. Human intuition is well-calibrated for linear addition, badly miscalibrated for exponential growth.
