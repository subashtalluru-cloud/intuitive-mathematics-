Bowl or Dome — Watch Where the Curve Sits Relative to Its Own Tangent
Drag the tangent point along each curve. A concave-up curve (bowl) always stays ABOVE its own tangent line nearby; concave-down (dome) always stays BELOW. Watch this hold everywhere you drag to.
Concave Up: f(x)=x^2
Concave Down: f(x)=-x^2
This "curve above its own tangent" versus "curve below its own tangent" behavior is not merely a visual coincidence — it is precisely what a positive versus negative second derivative guarantees, everywhere along the curve.
