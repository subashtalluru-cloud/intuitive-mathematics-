A Sliding Ladder — Two Speeds, Linked by One Fixed Length
A 10-foot ladder leans against a wall. Drag the base outward — the top slides DOWN, but at a genuinely different, non-constant speed, linked by the Pythagorean relationship x^2+y^2=100 at every instant.
A common mistake is substituting the known numerical values (x=6, y=8) into the equation BEFORE differentiating — this destroys the variables' relationship the derivative needs to act on, producing a meaningless result.
