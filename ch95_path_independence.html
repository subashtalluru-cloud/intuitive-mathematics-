Two Wildly Different Routes, Same Endpoints — Identical Integral
Integrating f(z)=z from 0 to 1+i. Click each path and watch the numerical result land at exactly the same place, regardless of the detour taken.
This path-independence has no real-variable analog whatsoever — and it is exactly what forces Cauchy's integral theorem's "closed loop gives zero" conclusion, since a closed loop is just a path from a point back to itself.
