The Law of Cosines Needs the INCLUDED Angle
Triangle with sides 40m and 60m. Which angle can you legitimately use in the Law of Cosines formula?
This is the chapter's own flagged common mistake: using an angle that isn't included between the two known sides. The Law of Cosines formula c²=a²+b²−2ab·cos(C) specifically requires C to be the angle BETWEEN sides a and b — using any other angle silently produces a wrong answer.
