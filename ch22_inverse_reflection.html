The Inverse Is a Mirror Image Across y=x
f(x) = 2x + 1. Click points on f's graph — watch their reflection appear on f⁻¹'s graph, across the diagonal mirror line y=x.
If f sends 1→3, then f⁻¹ must send 3→1 — literally swapping the input and output. Graphically, swapping x and y coordinates is exactly reflection across the line y=x.
