Why "Close Enough" Isn't — Parabola vs. Sphere
A common engineering shortcut approximates a dish as a section of a sphere, which is easier to manufacture. Fire the same 4 rays at both shapes and compare where they actually land.
✓ True Parabola
✗ Spherical Approximation
A dish shaped even slightly differently than a true parabola causes rays striking different parts to converge to slightly different points rather than one single, sharply-focused location — degrading exactly the signal the receiver is meant to collect.
