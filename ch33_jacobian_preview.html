A Cartographer's Real Problem — Distortion That Changes by Location
This map projection distorts area differently at every point — unlike Chapter 32's uniform matrices, a single global determinant can't capture it. Click anywhere on the LEFT map to see the local area-scaling factor AT THAT SPECIFIC POINT on the RIGHT.
This local, position-dependent determinant is called the Jacobian — Chapter 91 develops it fully, but the core idea is already fully visible here: the SAME determinant concept, just computed freshly at every different point instead of once for the whole transformation.
