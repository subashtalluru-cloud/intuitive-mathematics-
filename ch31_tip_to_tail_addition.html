Walk It Out — Vector Addition Is a Physical Path
Drag vector B's tip. Watch it automatically relocate to start where vector A ends — "tip-to-tail." The resultant (dashed) is your actual net displacement: walk A, then walk B from wherever that left you.
The tip-to-tail construction isn't an arbitrary geometric convention — it's a direct, literal picture of sequential physical displacement, which is exactly why vector addition reduces to simple independent addition of components.
