Gauss's Identity — A Proof Without Words
Claim: 1 + 2 + 3 + ... + n = n(n+1)/2. Instead of algebra, watch the staircase of dots pair with its own upside-down copy to form a clean rectangle.
n = 5
The staircase alone has 1+2+...+n dots. Pairing it with its own upside-down copy always makes a perfect n × (n+1) rectangle — twice the sum you wanted. Divide by 2, and the formula falls out with no algebra at all.
