Same Starting Height, Different Starting Velocity — Wildly Different Futures

Knowing an oscillating system's current position alone is genuinely insufficient to predict its future behavior — a mass at a specific height could be momentarily at rest there, or passing rapidly through that height, and only its velocity resolves the ambiguity.
