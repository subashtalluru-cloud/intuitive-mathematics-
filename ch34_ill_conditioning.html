When "Technically Invertible" Is Still Dangerous
Drag the slider to make matrix A nearly (but not exactly) singular. Watch what a microscopic 0.0001 change to the input b does to the solved answer as det(A) approaches zero.
A near-zero determinant is a direct, early warning sign of dangerous error-amplification — not merely a theoretical edge case, but a genuine, practically consequential numerical hazard in real engineering computation.
