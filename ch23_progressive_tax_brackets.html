Progressive Tax Brackets — Piecewise in the Real World
10% on the first $10,000, 20% on the next $30,000, 30% above that. Enter an income and watch each bracket's contribution as a stacked bar.
A common misconception: "earning more can put your WHOLE income in a higher bracket." False — each bracket only taxes its own slice, visible as separate stacked segments below, never the whole bar turning the top bracket's color.
