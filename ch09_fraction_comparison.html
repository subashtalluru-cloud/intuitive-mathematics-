Which Fraction Is Bigger? Test Your Instinct
"Bigger denominator always means smaller number" — is that actually always true? Compare these pairs using cross-multiplication and find out.
Cross-multiplication (a/b vs c/d: compare a×d vs c×b) always gives the right answer instantly — engineers use this exact trick to recognize equivalent gear ratios like 3:1 and 12:4 without re-deriving anything.
