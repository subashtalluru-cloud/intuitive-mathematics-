Rotate However You Like — the Determinant Refuses to Budge
Drag the angle all the way around a full circle. The parallelogram's shape and orientation change completely — but watch the determinant readout: it never leaves exactly 1.000, at every single angle.
A rotation, by its own physical nature, changes a shape's orientation and position but never its actual area — so its own scaling factor must, by this physical reasoning alone, equal exactly 1.
