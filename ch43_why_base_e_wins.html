Every Other Base Carries Extra Baggage — e Alone Travels Light
Click each base and watch its own derivative formula. Every base except e picks up an extra multiplying constant, ln(base) — a leftover of exactly how far that base's own growth rate strays from matching its own height.
Applying the power rule to 2^x — treating it as if the exponent were fixed and the base variable — is exactly the common mistake this chapter warns against, confusing two structurally different kinds of functions.
