Is This Pricing Scheme Truly Linear?
A ride costs $3 base + $2/mile for the first 5 miles, then $4/mile beyond. Test the rate at different mileages.
Recognizing whether a real situation is genuinely, fully linear (one constant rate everywhere) or only piecewise-linear (constant within sub-ranges but changing at a breakpoint) is a crucial modeling judgment call — get it wrong and a single formula silently produces wrong answers past the breakpoint.
