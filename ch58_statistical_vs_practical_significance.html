Statistically Significant ≠ Practically Meaningful
Click each scenario. One has a huge sample detecting a trivial difference; the other has a small sample failing to confirm a genuinely large one. Neither p-value alone tells the full story — Cohen's d does.

Effect size measures like Cohen's d provide a sample-size-independent measure of how large a difference genuinely is, distinct from whether that difference is statistically detectable.
