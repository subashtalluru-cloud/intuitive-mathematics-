sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 Is Just Pythagoras in Disguise
Drag the angle. Watch the right triangle formed by the swept point, the origin, and its projection — this IS the Pythagorean theorem, wearing new clothes.
This isn't an independent trigonometric fact requiring its own proof — it's the ordinary Pythagorean theorem, observed from a new angle (quite literally). Hypotenuse=1 (radius), legs=cos(θ) and sin(θ), so legs² sum = hypotenuse² = 1.
