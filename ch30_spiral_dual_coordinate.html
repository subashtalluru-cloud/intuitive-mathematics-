One Spiral, Two Coordinate Systems — Same Curve, Wildly Different Effort
Watch the identical spiral get traced simultaneously in polar coordinates (left, sweep-angle style) and rectangular coordinates (right, x-y style). Same curve. Notice which panel's equation actually explains what's happening.
Polar: r = θ
One simple rule: distance grows exactly as fast as angle sweeps
Rectangular: same curve
No simple y=f(x) form — x and y are joint functions of θ, awkward to write directly
A coordinate system's usefulness depends on how well its own natural structure matches the symmetry of the curve being described — polar coordinates naturally suit rotational, center-outward shapes exactly like this one.
