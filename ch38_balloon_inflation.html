Constant Air Flow, Slowing Radius Growth
A balloon inflates at a CONSTANT volume rate (100 cubic cm per second, always). Drag the current radius and watch how fast the radius itself is growing at that instant — it slows down dramatically as the balloon gets bigger, even though the air flow never changes.
A common mistake is substituting the known numerical radius into the volume equation BEFORE differentiating — this destroys the variable relationship the derivative needs, producing a meaningless constant instead of a genuine rate.
